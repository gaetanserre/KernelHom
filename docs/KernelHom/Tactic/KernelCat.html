    Kernel category tactics #

    This file implements tactics which apply the kernel_hom transformation and then use a categorical tactic to solve the resulting goal.

    Main declarations #

    • kernel_disch: kernel_hom followed by monoidal or cat_disch, after normalizing the tensor products of morphisms. It is the tactic to use by default.
    • kernel_monoidal: kernel_hom followed by monoidal.
    • kernel_coherence: kernel_hom followed by coherence.
    • aesop_kernel: kernel_hom followed by aesop with the CategoryTheory rule set, skipping the rfl_cat attempt of cat_disch.

          def kernelDisch :
          Lean.ParserDescr

          The kernel_disch tactic applies the kernel_hom transformation to the goal and then tries to solve the resulting goal with:

          1. monoidal, after simp together with the splitting of the tensor products of compositions and the coassociativity of the comultiplication;
          2. cat_disch, after merging the whiskers and the compositions of tensor products into single tensor products, together with the counit and comultiplication laws of the comonoid structure and of the deterministic and Markov kernels. This handles the exchange law and the laws hidden in tensor products.

          As it tries both cat_disch and monoidal, it is the tactic to use by default.
