    Kernel transformation utilities #

    Explicit constructors for the objects and morphisms of SFinKer used by the kernel_hom and hom_kernel tactics. The applications are built directly (mkAppN with explicit universe levels and cached instances) instead of going through mkAppM.

    Main declarations #

    • unfoldKernelOp: unfolds Kernel.prod and Kernel.compProd.
    • SFinKerInsts: the category-theoretic instances of SFinKer.{u}.
    • computeSFinkerOf, idME: the object of SFinKer associated with a measurable space, and the identity measurable equivalence, recursively on products (memoized).
    • mkCatComp, mkTensorHom, ...: explicit constructors of morphisms.
