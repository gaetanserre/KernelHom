        Recursive translation of a kernel e : Kernel Unit X into a measure e'. Returns e' together with a proof of e = Kernel.const Unit e'. Each step is a single application of a translation lemma, given the translations of the subterms (Kernel.comp_const_of_eq, ...). Only the kernels built by measure_kernel are recognized.

          def MeasureKernel.kernelMeasure :
          Lean.ParserDescr

          The kernel_measure tactic is the inverse of measure_kernel: it transforms an equality of kernels from Unit back to an equivalent equality of measures.

          The tactic supports location specifiers like rw or simp:

          • kernel_measure — applies to the goal
          • kernel_measure at h — applies to hypothesis h
          • kernel_measure at h₁ h₂ — applies to multiple hypotheses
          • kernel_measure at h ⊢ — applies to hypothesis h and the goal
          • kernel_measure at * — applies to all hypotheses and the goal

          It is useful to switch back to measure equations once kernel rewrites are done.
