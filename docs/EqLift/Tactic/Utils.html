    Lift and Unlift utilities #

    This file provides utility functions for lifting and unlifting equalities.

    A lifting (resp. unlifting) function transforms an expression e into an expression e' living in a common universe level (resp. in the original universe levels), together with a proof that the expression in the common universe level is the lift of the other one: e' = lift e when lifting, e = lift e' when unlifting. These proofs are combined by congruence to obtain the equivalence between the original equality and the transformed one.
