    Lean.MetaM ((κ : Q(ProbabilityTheory.Kernel «$X» «$Y»)) × (κ' : Q(ProbabilityTheory.Kernel «$X'» «$Y'»)) × Q(«$κ'» = «$κ».lift))

    Typed version of the lifting of a kernel κ : Kernel X Y by the registered lifting functions. Given the measurable equivalences ex : X' ≃ᵐ X and ey : Y' ≃ᵐ Y returns κ, its lift κ' and the proof of κ' = κ.lift.
